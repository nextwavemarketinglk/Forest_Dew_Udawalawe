# Image credits / responsible publishing

## Genuine hotel photographs
Thirteen images were supplied directly by the hotel project owner and color-graded for web display. They show:
1. `hotel-aerial-canopy.jpg` — High-altitude aerial drone perspective showing Forest Dew nestled inside the emerald forest canopy bordering the Udawalawe lake/reservoir.
2. `hotel-exterior-facade.jpg` — Two-storey boutique white guest wing with private glass balconies, shaded ground-floor verandas, wooden loungers, and tropical elephant ear flora.
3. `room-double-romantic.jpg` — Deluxe Double Room showing authentic folded swan towel origami adorned with fresh tropical flower petals, dual bedside nightstands, and soft ambient wall sconces.
4. `room-single-terracotta-full.jpg` — Single Room Suite overview featuring the warm terracotta feature wall, ceiling light fixture, nightstand, and comfortable king bed.
5. `room-single-desk-view.jpg` — Single Room in-room amenities showing dedicated writing/vanity desk, electric kettle, complimentary mineral water bottles, and ochre floral bed runner.
6. Actual guest rooms (`guest-room-forest.webp`, `guest-room-wide.webp`, `guest-room-lounge.webp`).
7. Outdoor freshwater swimming pool (`swimming-pool.webp`).
8. Open timber dining terrace & restaurant interior (`restaurant-tables.webp`, `restaurant-interior.webp`).
9. Canopy terrace & balcony (`forest-balcony.webp`).
10. Welcome reception desk (`hotel-reception.webp`).

No furniture, structural elements, or building features were fabricated.

## Excluded people photos
Photographs of two individual guests and a swimming pool scene featuring identifiable children and adults are **not published** in the V3 website package. Secure explicit publication permissions before adding them.

## Illustrative concept artwork
`hero-forest-elephants.webp` and its mobile derivative were generated for the previous version. They are illustrative and not documentary photographs of the hotel or a specific wildlife sighting. The site labels this artwork.

## Rights
Before public publishing, hotel owner should confirm rights to share all submitted property photos and logo.
