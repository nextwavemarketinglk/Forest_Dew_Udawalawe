Our Sanctuary

A Haven Beside the Wild.

Forest Dew was created to offer an intimate, personal alternative to large impersonal tourist hotels.

Located in Udawalawe in the Sabaragamuwa province, our property provides a tranquil base bordering the lake and wildlife buffer. Our emblem—depicting a mother elephant and her calf under a tropical tree and rising golden sun—embodies our deep connection to this land.

The Experience

Unhurried Hospitality.

We believe the finest travel memories are made not through lavish excess, but through thoughtful care, peaceful spaces, and genuine human warmth.

Whether it’s packing a fresh picnic breakfast for your early morning safari, preparing a cool towel upon your return, or ensuring your room is a calm, quiet sanctuary for deep sleep, our team takes pride in attentive, unobtrusive service.
