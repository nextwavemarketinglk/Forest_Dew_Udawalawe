Water & Stillness

The Poolside Sanctuary.

Under the southern Sri Lankan sun, our outdoor swimming pool is a natural centerpiece for daily relaxation.

Framed by tropical garden plants and palm fronds, it offers cool respite after dusty game drives across the park. Bring a book, take an unhurried swim, and let the afternoon pass without an itinerary.

Local Flavors

Gathering at the Table.

Food at Forest Dew celebrates Sri Lankan home-style culinary traditions and fresh local produce.

Start early with fresh tropical fruits and hot Ceylon tea before your dawn safari departure. In the evening, enjoy hearty rice and curry banquets, aromatic spiced vegetable dishes, and unhurried dining with fellow travelers.

Canopy Views

Treetop Vistas.

Our nature-facing balcony looks out directly onto the surrounding green tree canopy and village foliage.

It is the premier vantage point on the property for birdwatching during dawn and sunset. Keep your binoculars ready for hornbills, parakeets, kingfishers, and iridescent sunbirds darting through the leaves.

Outdoor Spaces

Verandas & Lush Lawns.

Step directly from your suite into shaded verandas and an emerald garden framed by giant tropical elephant ear plants.

Furnished with handcrafted timber recliners, the ground terrace offers a breezy setting for afternoon reading, quiet tea moments, and letting the clean jungle breeze wash over you after hours exploring the national park.
