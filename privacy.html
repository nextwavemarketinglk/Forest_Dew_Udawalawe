Zero Data Harvesting

How Enquiries Work

This website functions as a clean static presentation hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It does not employ tracking cookies, third-party analytics trackers, or server-side customer databases. When you submit a booking or contact enquiry, your web browser formats the message and opens WhatsApp directly to communicate with Forest Dew (+94 76 638 6132). Information is transmitted only when you hit send inside WhatsApp, subject to WhatsApp’s own standard encryption and privacy terms.

Pricing Integrity

Rates, Currencies & Reservations

All room rates are quoted in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and safari packages in US Dollars (USD). Accommodation rates and safari costs are kept strictly separate and never artificially converted. Submitting an enquiry via this website does not constitute a guaranteed reservation or require an online financial transaction. The hotel management will verify room availability, season policies, applicable taxes, and final prices with you directly.
