Natural Light & Space

Designed for Restoration.

Every room at Forest Dew is created to offer a tranquil counterpoint to the energetic heat and dusty excitement of the national park.

Wide glass doors open onto private balconies and shaded garden verandas, welcoming soft morning sun and green foliage views, while generous ceiling heights ensure continuous airflow through tropical days.

  • ✦ Warm terracotta feature wall with ambient evening sconces
  • ✦ Dedicated in-room tea kettle, mineral water & work vanity
  • ✦ Clean tiled floors cool underfoot after hours on safari
  • ✦ Private en-suite bathroom with fresh water amenities
