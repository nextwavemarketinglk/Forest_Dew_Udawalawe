Safari Transparency Notice

All package rates quoted in US Dollars (USD) represent total group charges (not per-person). Published package tiers are configured strictly for 2 or 4 guests. Safari vehicles are private to your party. While Udawalawe supports one of the highest densities of wild Asian elephants in the world, sightings are governed by natural wild behavior and cannot be artificially guaranteed.

Practical Insights

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is our safari jeep private?

Yes. All packages supplied by Forest Dew include an exclusive, private 4x4 safari vehicle for you and your travel companions.

Are park entrance tickets included?

Yes. Official national park entry permits, bottled drinking water, and binoculars are fully included in all three packages.

What are the best safari departure times?

Early morning (around 5:30 – 6:00 AM) and mid-afternoon (around 2:30 – 3:00 PM) offer the most pleasant temperatures and highest wildlife movement.

How do I book and pay?

Submit an enquiry via WhatsApp or our Plan Your Stay planner. The hotel team will confirm availability, departure times, and payment details with you directly.
